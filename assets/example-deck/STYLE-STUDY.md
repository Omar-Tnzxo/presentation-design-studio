# Style study: "UI/UX Design Portfolio" by Fatma El Mahdy (Behance, Oct 2026)

Source: https://www.behance.net/gallery/257232983/UIUX-Design-Portfolio (54 slides, 1400 px wide).
Measured from local renders of every slide (contact sheets + full-size crops) and pixel sampling.
All sizes below are converted to our 1920 x 1080 canvas (x1.37 from the 1400 px source).

## 1. Background and light
- Base is near pure black: #000104 (intro), #040100 (orange sections), #000202 (teal sections). 35-78% of every slide is this black.
- Light is a soft, silky aurora / light-leak: long blurred curved bands entering from the edges, never centred blobs. Strongest at one or two edges, leaving the centre black for content.
- The glow colours are DEEP and saturated, not bright:
  - Intro / blue: #001E57, #000E55, #003A61, #006EA0, #0084A9, highlights #009BC1.
  - Orange sections: dark amber #5E3C00, #8E5301 (the glow), never neon orange.
  - Teal sections: #15585D, #3D898F, highlight #68DAE7.
- Each section owns one light colour (blue intro, orange project 1, brown project 2, green project 4, teal project 6). Slides inside a section keep it.

## 2. Typography (source fonts: a condensed grotesk like Bebas/Anton + Poppins)
| Role | Source look | Size on 1920 | Our Arabic font |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero word (PORTFOLIO) | ultra condensed, all caps, white | cap height ~470 px, spans ~88% of the width | Mohawla Typeface |
| Section words (ABOUT ME, CONTENTS, EDUCATION) | same condensed face | cap height ~140 px | Mohawla Typeface |
| Card titles (HOME FIX, GOAL AXIS) | same condensed face | cap height ~80 px | Mohawla Typeface |
| Project bar text (PROJECT 1 / MOBILE APP) | condensed, caps, with a vertical bar divider | ~60 px | Mohawla Typeface |
| Slide titles (Design Thinking Process, Target Audience) | Poppins SemiBold, top-left | ~44 px | Tajawal ExtraBold |
| Big numbers (01, 02, 03) | Poppins Bold | ~120 px | Tajawal ExtraBold |
| Card labels (Problem, Impact) | Poppins Bold | ~46 px | Tajawal Bold |
| Body text | Poppins Regular/Medium, white, bold keywords inside sentences | 30-50 px (it is LARGE) | Tajawal Medium |
| Small labels (01 . EMPATHIZE) | Inter/Poppins SemiBold caps | ~38 px | Tajawal Bold |
- Text is pure white; secondary text is still white (no grey body copy). Emphasis = bold words inside the sentence.
- Alignment: everything starts at one edge (left in the source, right for us). Centred only inside process steps.

## 3. Layout
- Outer margin ~ 4% (about 75 px). Titles sit at the top start corner.
- Content is big and fills the slide: cards, panels and devices are large; there are no tiny thumbnails.
- Devices overflow their cards (phones stick out above the card edge, food items float outside).

## 4. Components
- **Section bar** ("PROJECT 1 | MOBILE APP"): a pill that starts at the slide edge, filled with the section colour and fading to transparent along its length; condensed caps text with a "|" divider.
- **Gradient cards** (Problem/Impact/Solution, Personas): radius ~50 px, vertical gradient lit from the BOTTOM: top #010102 -> middle #2A3B50 -> bottom #24476B (navy). The highlighted card: top #763F18 -> middle #DC9B6A -> bottom #F2883D (orange). No borders, no outlines.
- **Staggered cards** with a thick dashed white arrow (dash ~30 px, 4 px stroke, solid triangle head) looping under the cards to the highlighted one.
- **Process panel** (Design Thinking): one wide rounded panel (radius ~50 px), gradient top #AD5E27 -> middle #000001 -> bottom #214266. Inside: icon circles ~230 px wide, peach gradient #FEEFE8 -> #FFD7C1, orange line icons (#E8833D, ~2.4 stroke); peach chevrons between circles; centred labels "01 . NAME" and 2-4 short lines under each.
- **Content cards on the contents slide**: translucent panels (the aurora shows through: #22699A where light is behind, #011F27 where it is dark), radius ~50 px, condensed title + body + large tilted device mockups.
- **Device mockups**: realistic 3D iPhones (silver or black titanium), tilted 15-25 degrees, often two overlapping; laptops for web work.
- **Real photography / 3D renders** carry the hero slots (portrait cut-out over the hero word; 3D characters; lifestyle mockup scenes). The hero object overlaps the hero word but the word stays readable.
- **Design system slide**: colour swatches as big rounded tiles or circles with hex + role, typography specimen "Aa".
- **Flow charts**: dark rounded pills (brown/navy gradient) joined by thin arrows; numbered tiles 01-04 stepping diagonally.

## 5. What this means for our deck
- Use deep, edge-entering light bands per section (blue for intro/contents, amber + navy for pricing and process).
- Make every type size 1.5-2x what a normal deck uses; body text 30-36 px minimum.
- Cards lit from the bottom, no borders. Highlight = orange gradient card.
- Devices large and overflowing; a 3D hero object plays the role of the portrait on the cover.
- Mirror everything for RTL: start edge = right.
